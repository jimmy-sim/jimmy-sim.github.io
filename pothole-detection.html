Pothole Detection Under Motion Blur

Coauthored research · April–October 2024

I coauthored Enhancing Pothole Detection Robustness to Blurred Images and Real-Time Implementation on Embedded Boards with Taehee Kim. The project addresses a practical challenge: motion blur can make road damage harder to detect from a moving vehicle.

Read CJSJ submission (PDF)Download PDF

2024–25 Columbia Junior Science Journal submission · Semifinalist.

Approach

The system pairs an image-deblurring network with a pothole detector. The deblurring stage uses pixel-level L1 loss and a VGG19-based perceptual loss. Evaluation compares five detector architectures on clear, blurred, and deblurred images.

The paper combines Roboflow and Google Bangkit 2021 pothole datasets with an 80/20 training/test split. For embedded deployment, the team selected YOLOv5 for its practical computational requirements and implemented the system on an NVIDIA Jetson board.
