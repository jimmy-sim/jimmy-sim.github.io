About

I am a freshman at the University of California, Berkeley, studying Applied Mathematics and intending to double major in Computer Science. My interests include optimization, machine learning, and quantitative finance.

I work on conic quadratic optimization in Rust, mentored by Matteo Santamaria at UC Berkeley. Previously, I worked on machine learning and distributed systems, including a research internship at UC Santa Cruz.

Outside academics, I enjoy golf, film, poker, reading, prediction markets, and writing poetry.

Technical interests

I work with Python, C++, Rust, Haskell, R, and TensorFlow/Keras. My interests also include statistical modeling, game theory, and market making.

High-school leadership

Programming and student initiatives

I led the programming clubs at Korea International School, teaching algorithms and C++ to more than 40 students. I organized the International School Programming Cup and SoriHack, an AI and music hackathon, and founded Phoenix Talks, a speaker series with more than 40 interviews and panels.

Quiz Bowl and athletics

I captained Quiz Bowl to a KAIAC title and the 2025 Asian Championships. As cross-country and track captain, I helped the teams earn national and international podium finishes and set two school relay records.

Teaching and community service

Schoolhouse.world

I hosted more than 200 math, science, and SAT sessions for over 250 learners across 45 countries, and facilitated group discussions on social issues and AI.

Tutor profile

Helping Hands and the Memory Keepers Project

Through Helping Hands, I tutored English and coding to more than 120 children and seniors. I also founded the Memory Keepers Project, archiving over 200 primary sources on families separated by the Korean War and sharing them through library workshops.

Writing and journalism

I led more than 50 student journalists at The ISK Times and organized student publications and AI literacy events through SAILING.

A Commended Poet in the 2025 Foyle Young Poets of the Year Award, I have published in Blue Marble Review and attended the Iowa and Ithaca summer writing programs.
